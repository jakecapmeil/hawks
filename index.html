Watch sync
Pulls your Garmin or COROS runs into your log automatically through intervals.icu (free). No Claude or Strava needed.
  1. Make a free account at intervals.icu and connect Garmin or COROS there (Settings → Connections). Don't use its Strava link: Strava won't share those runs.
  2. On intervals.icu, open Settings. Copy your athlete id (looks like i12345) and, under Developer Settings, your API key.
  3. Paste both here. Hawks checks them, then syncs about every hour.
